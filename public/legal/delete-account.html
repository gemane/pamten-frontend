Delete Your Account

Applies to the Owlgraph website and the Owlgraph mobile app

You can delete your Owlgraph account yourself, at any time, without asking us. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no grace period and no recovery.

In the app or on the website

  1. Sign in at dev.owlgraph.org or in the mobile app.
  2. Open Settings (the gear icon).
  3. Go to Delete account.
  4. Enter your password to confirm. We ask for it so that a stolen session alone cannot destroy your account.

You are signed out immediately and the account is gone.

By email

If you cannot sign in — you have lost your password and your two-factor device, say — write to {{OWNER_EMAIL}} from the email address on the account. We may need to confirm it is really you before deleting, since we cannot let one person delete another's account. We reply within one month, and in practice much sooner.

What is deleted

Deleted permanentlyKept, but anonymised
  • Your account record and email address
  • Your password hash
  • Your two-factor secret and recovery codes
  • All your sessions, on every device
  • Your rate-limit counters
  • Reports you filed about incorrect company data — the link back to you is severed, so they can no longer be connected to you

Why reports survive. A report says something about a company's data, not about you. Deleting reports outright would silently rewrite the moderation history and could restore errors that were already fixed. So we cut the link to you and keep the correction. If you would rather a specific report were removed entirely, ask us and we will look at it.

What deleting your account does not do

Owlgraph's ownership graph is built from public registers, not from user accounts. If you appear in that graph as a company director or a person with significant control, that is separate data and deleting an account does not touch it — you may never have had an account at all. To exercise your rights over that data, see Part 2 of the privacy policy, or write to {{OWNER_EMAIL}}.
