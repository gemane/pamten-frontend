Editorial line (§25 MedienG)

Owlgraph is a non-commercial, open-source project that makes company ownership and control structures visible by combining public registers into a single searchable graph. It publishes factual data drawn from official sources and states the source of every record. It pursues no political, religious or commercial agenda, carries no advertising, and is not funded by any of the companies it describes.

Online dispute resolution

The European Commission provides a platform for online dispute resolution at ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr. We are neither obliged nor willing to take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration body.

Liability for content and links

Owlgraph reproduces data from public registers. We take care that it is imported faithfully and attributed to its source, but we do not guarantee that the registers themselves are accurate, complete or current — see the Terms of Use. External links are checked when added; we have no influence over their later content and accept no liability for it. If you find something wrong, please use the report button in the app or write to us.
