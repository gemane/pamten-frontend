1. What the service is

Owlgraph imports company ownership records from public registers and presents them as a searchable graph. It is a reference tool. It is not legal, financial, compliance or investment advice, and it is not a substitute for checking the underlying register.

2. Accuracy — please read this one

The data is reproduced from third-party registers and provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. It may be incomplete, out of date, wrongly matched, or simply wrong at the source. Ownership structures also change faster than registers record them.

Two specific caveats worth knowing:

  • Records from different registers are matched by shared identifiers and by name. Matching by name can be wrong — two different companies or people can share one.
  • Absence of a relationship is not evidence that none exists. Many jurisdictions publish little or nothing.

Before you rely on anything here for a decision that matters, verify it against the source register. Every record links to its source.
