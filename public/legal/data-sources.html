Last updated: 9 August 2026

Owlgraph holds no ownership data of its own. Everything comes from the public registers below, and every record in the app links back to the source it came from. Several of these licences require attribution, so this page is a licence obligation rather than a courtesy — and if you reuse our database, these conditions travel with it.

How the data is combined

Records describing the same company are merged when they share a hard identifier — an LEI, a Companies House number, an SEC CIK or a Wikidata QID. Where no shared identifier exists, matching falls back to normalised names, which is less reliable; such merges are reviewed rather than applied automatically. Each source carries a credibility score, and where two sources disagree about the same fact, the more credible and more recent one is shown. Every value keeps a record of which source asserted it.

Corrections

If a record here is wrong, the report button in the app sends it to a moderation queue — no account needed. Please also consider correcting it at the source register: we re-import from those registers regularly, so a fix at the source is the one that lasts, and it helps everyone else using the same data.
